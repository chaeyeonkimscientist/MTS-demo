Hi Brent! You said you want to grow MTS on two sides:

  • Media growth: incubate shows across new verticals, generating raw materials
  • Product growth: build an artifact people can use the media materials with

I think these goals are interconnected, and there is one concrete product that brings the two goals together. I’ll elaborate below.

THE PREMISE

The premise: it's a memory problem, not a coverage problem

Upon reading some news coverage & critic articles, I realized that most feedback treats MTS as a distribution problem: reach, speed, and virality.

Coming from cognitive science and content distribution, I think this is a misdiagnosis.

MTS relies on X which already delivers the information faster than any show.

X, by its product design, does not prioritise consolidation, so information does not convert into understanding you keep.

Thus sensemaking (whilst MTS is using X) is more of a salience problem, less a distribution one.

MEDIA GROWTH

First, Media growth

Before you own coverage on AI, crypto, geopolitics etc., you’d have to prioritize 1-2 realms.

I did a quick research on real-time firehoses people are emotionally invested in and can't parse alone, here are the two.

PRODUCT GROWTH

Second, a Product that takes it from there

When you asked which company I think is cool, I name-dropped Flora.

And when I asked what growth looks like from the product side, you described a Bloomberg-esque product that has people build meaning from the information they got.

In order to build a product that lets people retain the information from shows, I would borrow the shape from Flora’s node-canvas and point it at news instead of images. Every story becomes a node.

show airs → stories become nodes → connections drawn → a graph you keep

Why? Memory consolidates through connection, so the act of linking two pieces of information retains them, which makes it great cognitive architecture for sensemaking. Flora never made that argument, and it’s time distribution does it.

And it solves the empty-canvas trap. Most people won’t build a graph from scratch, so the show does the first pass. Hosts and producers publish each day’s stories with the connections already drawn. The user will then fork it, extend the threads they care about, and build a view over time with a light AI layer suggesting links (on-canvas assistance). This will act as a custom catalog for the information overload coming from an always-on stream.

THE THROUGH-LINE

The through-line: the two sides are one pipeline

This product will generate the following pipeline: shows generate the graph → graph makes show durable and personal. For MTS, what you cover determines what the product can connect, and how people use the product tells you what to cover next.
